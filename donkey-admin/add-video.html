Before you upload

  • MP4 only, 60 seconds or shorter.
  • Shrink every video first with HandBrake (free): preset General → Fast 720p30, format MP4, video codec H.264.
  • Aim for under 8 MB per video (hard limit 25 MB). Smaller files save bandwidth on the free Supabase plan.
  • A still preview picture is made automatically from the start of each video.
  • Optional description (max 100 characters) under each video. It shows on the video in the app, in Reel mode, in My Jokes, and is searchable.
