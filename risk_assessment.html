About this document

This is Donkey App's written illegal content risk assessment, children's access assessment and children's risk assessment under the UK Online Safety Act 2023, following Ofcom's four-step method: understand the harms, assess the risk, decide measures, report and review.

Ratings use Ofcom's levels: Negligible, Low, Medium, High. Each rating considers how likely the harm is on Donkey App and how serious it would be, after the measures in place.

Service description

Donkey App is a small comedy service where people read, rate and share jokes, memes and short videos. It has no private messaging, no live streaming and no comments.

How users can share content

Feature Who can use it How it goes live
Jokes (text) Anyone, account not needed (app or website) Only after it is approved by hand
Memes (images) Signed-in users who confirm they are 18 or over, app only After approval; "trusted" uploaders (verified in person or by video call) go live straight away
Videos (max 90 seconds) Same as memes Same as memes
Profile: display name, picture, about me, website, social links, events Signed-in users Live straight away
Following other users Signed-in users Live straight away; follower lists can be hidden by the owner
Ratings (votes) Anyone Counts only; voters are not stored

Users and ages

  • Minimum age 13; under-18s need a parent's or guardian's permission. Uploading memes and videos needs a declaration of being 18 or over.
  • Age is self-declared, not verified.

Safety measures in place

  • Manual approval of every joke, meme and video (except trusted uploaders).
  • Terms and Conditions with a zero-tolerance list: nudity or sexual content, anything involving children, violence and self-harm, hate, harassment, people shown without consent, others' material, deepfakes, illegal content, spam.
  • Report button on every post.
  • Block a user or hide a post; blocked users' posts disappear from all feeds.
  • Accounts can be stopped from uploading; limit of 20 uploads per person per 24 hours.
  • Account deletion within 72 hours; Contact Us form and email for complaints.

Illegal content risk assessment

Overall risk is Low: there is no private messaging, all jokes, memes and videos are checked by hand before going live, and the user base is small.

Risk factors that apply: image and video upload, user profiles, user connections (following), links to other sites, posts by unregistered users (jokes), and users aged 13–17. Risk factors that do not apply: private messaging, group chat, livestreaming, anonymous direct contact, payments between users, user-generated search, encrypted messaging.

Children's access assessment

Conclusion: Donkey App is likely to be accessed by children, so a children's risk assessment is carried out below.

  1. Is it possible for children to use the service? Yes. Users from 13 are allowed, and there is no highly effective age check.
  2. Does the child user condition apply? Yes. A free comedy app with memes and short videos appeals to teenagers, and 13–17-year-olds are allowed.

Children's risk assessment

Overall risk to children is Medium, driven by adult humour and bullying-style jokes, not by dangerous content. The main protection is that every joke, meme and video is checked before children can see it.

Reporting, complaints and records

Reports and complaints are handled by the provider personally, aiming to act within 48 hours, and on illegal content involving children within 24 hours.

  • How to report: the Report button on every post, the Contact Us page, or email andy@donkeyapp.com.
  • What happens: the report is reviewed; illegal or banned content is removed and the account can be blocked. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the police and the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF).
  • Appeals: if you think your post was wrongly removed or your account wrongly blocked, complain through Contact Us and you will get a reply.
  • Records: this assessment and a log of serious reports and actions are kept and reviewed at least once a year and before any significant change to the service.
